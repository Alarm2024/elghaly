Refund policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What we sell

elghaly, LLC sells digital products: software seats (Plumb), reports and alerts (QUAY). There is nothing physical to send back.

The policy

If you want your money back, you get it. You do not need to give a reason and there is no form. There is no time limit: before delivery, after delivery, a week later or a month later.

You get back what you paid, once per purchase, in the same currency you paid in. A monthly plan can be cancelled at any time by email, and nothing more is charged after you cancel; a refund request can cover any payment.

How to ask

Email wyndham35@elghaly.dev or support@elghaly.dev from the address you used at checkout. One line is enough; add the order number if you have it. We reply to say when it is done.

How the money comes back

  • Paid through the shop checkout (PayPal or card): back to the same PayPal account or card, never to a different one. The checkout sends it the same day; your bank or PayPal decides when it lands, usually 5–10 business days.
  • Paid by wallet transfer: back to the wallet it came from, in the same asset and network. Each product's refund page has the details: Plumb refunds, QUAY refunds.

What a refund ends

A refund ends your licence or service for that purchase: updates, follow-up and support for it stop. It does not delete anything you built, and you can buy again later.

What this page is not

This is a refund policy, not a promise about results. Our software and reports are sold as they are, and nothing on our sites promises income or performance. See the terms.
