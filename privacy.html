Who we are

elghaly.dev is the website of elghaly, run by elghaly, LLC, 548 Market St, San Francisco, CA 94104, United States. Questions about this policy: wyndham35@elghaly.dev.

What this site collects

This site has no user accounts and no sign-in. It does not hold funds or assets for anyone, and no payments are taken on elghaly.dev itself.

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to see how many people visit and which pages they view. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects standard data such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and referring site. We do not use it to identify you personally. You can learn how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Fonts are served from this site. The home page also loads an event registration button from Luma, which may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads. If you register for an event, that happens on Luma under Luma's own privacy policy.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email, order number and message you type are sent to our inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which handles them under its own privacy policy. We use them to reply to you.

Purchases

Purchases happen in the shop checkout, run by Shopify, with payment by PayPal or card. Shopify and PayPal process your order and payment details under their own privacy policies; the shop's privacy policy is linked at checkout. We receive what we need to deliver the order and to refund it.

Email

If you email us, we keep your message and address to reply to you. We do not sell or rent your information.

Other elghaly sites

Links on this site lead to other elghaly projects and to outside services. Each of those may have its own terms and privacy notice.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To ask about or remove any messages we hold, write to wyndham35@elghaly.dev.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
