Who we are

elghaly.dev is the website of the brand elghaly, run by Wyndham Heaven as a sole proprietor. Questions about this policy: wyndham35@elghaly.dev.

What this site collects

This site has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no forms. It does not hold funds or assets for anyone, and no payments are taken on elghaly.dev.

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to see how many people visit and which pages they view. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects standard data such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and referring site. We do not use it to identify you personally. You can learn how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Fonts are served from this site. The home page also loads an event registration button from Luma, which may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads. If you register for an event, that happens on Luma under Luma's own privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we keep your message and address to reply to you. We do not sell or rent your information.

Other elghaly sites

Links on this site lead to other elghaly projects and to outside services. Each of those may have its own terms and privacy notice.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To ask about or remove any email correspondence we hold, write to wyndham35@elghaly.dev.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
