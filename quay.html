Reviews, reports and alerts for people who run Solana bots.

What QUAY is

QUAY is for people who run Solana bots and want a second pair of eyes on their fees, tips and setup. You send what we ask for, such as transaction hashes or the feed to watch; you keep your wallet and your keys.

Monthly plans

  • QUAY Land — US$149 a month: a written report every week for one bot, by email: landed vs failed, and where tips are wasted. You send transaction hashes; you keep the wallet.
  • Node Watch — US$79 a month: a Telegram alert when an RPC or gRPC feed you name goes quiet. Alerts start once you have sent us which feed to watch.

Cancel a monthly plan at any time by email. Nothing more is charged after you cancel.

One-time

  • Fee review — US$199: a written review of your bot's fees, priority tips and setup costs, by email within 48 hours of a complete brief.
  • Score report — US$49: a written score of your setup against a fixed checklist, with your top fixes, by email.
  • Flex card pack — US$29: editable Telegram status/alert card templates, sent by email.

Delivery times are on the delivery page.

What QUAY is not

QUAY describes your own bot's activity and setup. We make no promise of results from any product, and we never hold your keys or your funds.

Refunds

If you want your money back, you get it: no reason needed and no time limit. See the refund policy.
